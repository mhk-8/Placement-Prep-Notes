# ML Interview Q&A Bank

## Why this matters
Your own curated answer bank, written in your own words. Rewriting someone else's answers is what makes them stick.

- [ ] 50 conceptual questions with 3-5 sentence answers
- [ ] 20 math derivations written out
- [ ] 15 'what would you do if...' scenario questions
- [ ] 10 questions specific to each of your projects
- [ ] Questions you were actually asked, added after every interview
