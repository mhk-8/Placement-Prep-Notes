# Probability & Statistics

> Folder: `05_AI_ML/02_Probability_and_Statistics`

## Why this matters
Also the backbone of quant-flavoured OA sections.

## Must-know checklist
- [ ] Probability axioms, conditional probability, Bayes theorem
- [ ] Random variables; PMF/PDF/CDF; expectation, variance, covariance
- [ ] Distributions: Bernoulli, binomial, Poisson, uniform, normal, exponential — and where each arises
- [ ] CLT and law of large numbers
- [ ] MLE vs MAP; likelihood functions
- [ ] Hypothesis testing: p-value, type I/II errors, power, t-test, chi-square
- [ ] Confidence intervals; bootstrapping
- [ ] A/B testing design and pitfalls
- [ ] Bayesian vs frequentist framing

## Online-assessment angle
Probability word problems and Bayes questions are standard in quant/aptitude sections of ML OAs.

## Interview angle
'Explain p-value to a non-technical stakeholder' and A/B test design questions are extremely common.
