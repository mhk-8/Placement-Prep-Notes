## 1. Tiering

Tier by **fit and probability**, not by brand. A tier-A list of twelve unreachable companies is a plan to be disappointed.

- **Tier A — stretch (3–5):** high bar, you would take it instantly. Worth dedicated prep even at low probability.
- **Tier B — core (6–10):** strong fit, realistic conversion, where most of your targeted prep belongs.
- **Tier C — floor (3–5):** you would accept it, conversion is likely. These exist so that the season has a floor and you can take Tier A risks calmly.

**Rule:** at least half your company-specific prep hours go to Tier B. Tier A gets the leftover enthusiasm; Tier C gets a folder and one revision pass.

## 4. Per-company data to collect — the checklist

For each shortlisted company, fill `08_Company_Wise/<Company>/00-process.md` with:

- [ ] Rounds and their order; how many people are cut at each
- [ ] OA platform, duration, number of sections, negative marking, whether you can revisit questions
- [ ] Whether the OA is proctored (webcam, tab-switch detection) and whether it is time-windowed
- [ ] Coding language options on their platform (some exclude Python)
- [ ] Interview format: DSA only, or DSA + design, or DSA + core CS
- [ ] Whether they ask ML/domain questions for your specific role
- [ ] CTC breakdown (base / stock / bonus — the headline number is usually not the base)
- [ ] Location and whether it is negotiable
- [ ] Bond / service agreement, if any
- [ ] Historical cutoff, if seniors know it
- [ ] Names of seniors who sat this company last cycle

**The single highest-return 30 minutes in this entire vault** is a conversation with a senior who sat your Tier-B companies last year. Do it in Week 0, not in November.

## 5. Prep allocation

Company-specific prep is a **5-day sprint**, run in the week before that company's OA. It never replaces the main plan.

| Day | Work |
|---|---|
| D-5 | Read `01-oa-pattern.md` and `02-past-questions.md` end to end. Identify their three most-repeated topics. |
| D-4, D-3 | Solve their past/similar questions, timed, in their format. |
| D-2 | Their core-CS or ML emphasis areas; skim their engineering blog for vocabulary. |
| D-1 | **Full simulation** in their exact format and duration. Then cheatsheets only. Early night. |

Run at most **two** company sprints in any week. Three means none of them is done properly.
