# Compilers & Theory of Computation

> Folder: `02_Core_CS/06_Compilers_and_Theory_of_Computation`

## Why this matters
Academic-heavy; appears in GATE-style OA sections and in research/core panels.

## Must-know checklist
- [ ] Compilation phases: lexical, syntax, semantic, IR, optimisation, codegen
- [ ] Regular expressions ↔ finite automata (DFA/NFA), pumping lemma
- [ ] CFGs, parse trees, ambiguity; LL(1) vs LR parsing
- [ ] Symbol table, activation records
- [ ] Static vs dynamic typing; call by value/reference/name
- [ ] Computability: halting problem, decidability, NP-completeness

## Online-assessment angle
DFA state-counting and grammar-classification MCQs are the usual form.

## Interview angle
Rarely deep, but 'is this problem NP-hard?' can come up when you propose a brute-force solution.
