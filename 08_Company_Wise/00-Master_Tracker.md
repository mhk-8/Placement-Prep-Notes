## 7. The Sunday review ⭐⭐

Fifteen minutes. Every week of the season.

```
□ Update the status board (§1)
□ Rewrite "This week" (§2)
□ Check: is every completed round debriefed?
□ Update "Where am I losing?" (§4) if anything closed
□ Read the folders for next week's companies
□ Pick ONE action from the accumulated debrief action lists and actually do it
□ Re-read your difficult-question answers if one of those companies is next
```

⚠️ **A tracker's failure mode is becoming a record instead of a tool.** The Sunday review is what
prevents that.
