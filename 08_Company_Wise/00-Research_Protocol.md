
# The Research Protocol

> **The highest-return thirty minutes of the placement season.** Run it the moment a company is
> announced, for every company, including ones with a folder already here (because last year's
> facts are not this year's).

---

## 1. The five steps ⭐⭐⭐

```
STEP 1 — PLACEMENT CELL  (10 min)
  Ask for, explicitly:
    □ the job description and the role title (not just "SDE")
    □ eligibility: CGPA cut-off, branch restrictions, backlog policy
    □ the process: how many rounds, which are eliminating
    □ the test platform and duration
    □ last year's process note, if they keep one
    □ CTC breakdown: base / joining bonus / stock / retention, and the service agreement
  ⚠️ Much of this is held but not published. Ask directly; do not assume it is unavailable.

STEP 2 — SENIORS  (10 min)
  Message 3-5 people from the last two batches who sat THIS company. Ask exactly six questions:
    1. Which platform, and was it proctored / webcam / tab-detection?
    2. How many sections, how long, and did sections LOCK?
    3. Negative marking?
    4. What were the coding problems ABOUT — topic, not statement?
    5. What was the hardest round, and what would you prepare differently?
    6. Roughly what attempt level / score got through?
  ⭐ Ask about the INTERVIEW rounds too, not just the OA. That is where the folder's value is.

STEP 3 — PUBLIC ARCHIVES  (5 min)
  □ GeeksforGeeks "<company> interview experience" — filter to the last 18 months
  □ LeetCode Discuss, company tag, sorted by recent
  □ Glassdoor interview questions
  □ The company's own careers page — many describe their process accurately
  ⚠️ Public sources are stale and skewed toward people who passed. Weight seniors higher.

STEP 4 — THE PLATFORM  (5 min)
  □ If it is Codility, HackerRank, iON or Mettl, do that platform's free demo test.
  ⭐ Genuinely worth it. The interface should not be new on test day.

STEP 5 — WRITE IT DOWN  (5 min)
  □ Copy `_TEMPLATE/` to `<Company>/` if it does not exist
  □ Fill or CORRECT the volatile table in `00-process-and-oa.md`, and date the correction
  □ Add dates and deadlines to `00-Master_Tracker.md`
  □ Decide which 2-3 projects you will lead with, and write it in the file
```

---

## 2. What to ask seniors, verbatim ⭐⭐

Copy-paste this. A specific, short message gets answered; a vague one does not.

```
Hi <name>, I'm <you>, M.Tech CSE '27. I saw you'd interviewed with <Company> — would you
mind sharing a bit about the process? Specifically:

1. Platform and duration for the OA, and whether sections locked?
2. Roughly what topics the coding questions were on?
3. How many interview rounds, and what each focused on?
4. Anything you'd prepare differently?

Completely fine if you're busy — even one-line answers would help a lot. Thanks!
```

⭐ **Then: write down what they tell you in the company folder, with their name and the date.** In
two months you will not remember which senior said what, and attribution matters when two sources
disagree.

⚠️ **Reciprocate.** Add your own debrief to the folder and share it with your juniors. The archive
you are benefiting from exists because someone did that.

---

## 3. The volatile-fact checklist ⭐⭐

These are the facts that change every year and that cost marks when wrong. Verify **all** of them
before every test.

```
□ Platform
□ Total duration
□ Number of sections
□ Do sections LOCK? Can you revisit questions?            ⚠️ costs the most when wrong
□ Negative marking, and on which sections
□ Calculator allowed?
□ Partial scoring on coding problems?                      ⚠️ changes your whole strategy
□ Languages permitted (is Python allowed?)
□ Webcam / proctoring / tab-switch detection
□ Eligibility: CGPA cut-off, backlog policy
□ Number and type of interview rounds
□ Whether there is a non-coding scored section (Amazon's Work Style, Wipro's essay)
```

---

## 4. Red flags worth noticing ⚠️

While researching, record these in the folder's "reservations" section. They matter if you end up
choosing between offers.

```
⚠️ A service agreement / bond with a large penalty, or longer than 2 years
⚠️ A role title that does not match the work described ("Systems Engineer" doing support)
⚠️ Vagueness about the team or the project you would join
⚠️ Very high reported attrition from your seniors
⚠️ A process that is consistently disorganised (rounds rescheduled, no communication)
⚠️ An interview that was inappropriate in any way — report to the placement cell  ⭐
```

⭐ Record the positives too. By December you will have forgotten which company's engineers you
actually enjoyed talking to, and that is genuinely predictive of whether you will be happy there.

---

## 5. Research quality rating ⭐

Rate each company folder's information so you know how much to trust it:

| Rating | Means |
|---|---|
| **High** | Your own attempt this season, or this year's campus notice |
| **Medium** | A senior from last year's batch, named and dated |
| **Low** | Public forum, undated, or inferred from a similar company |

Put the rating at the top of every `00-process-and-oa.md`. A file that does not say how confident
it is will be over-trusted.

---

## 6. The thirty-minute timer ⭐

```
 0-10  Placement cell
10-20  Seniors (send the messages — answers arrive later; do not wait)
20-25  Public archives
25-30  Platform demo + write it down
```

⚠️ **Send the senior messages first**, then do the other steps while you wait for replies. Blocking
on a reply is how the thirty minutes becomes three days and then never happens.
