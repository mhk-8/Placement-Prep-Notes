# Verbal Ability

> Folder: `06_Online_Assessments/04_Verbal_Ability`

## Why this matters
Low-effort, reliable marks. Common at service-based companies and in some product OAs.

## Must-know checklist
- [ ] Reading comprehension strategy (read questions first)
- [ ] Sentence correction and common grammar errors
- [ ] Para jumbles
- [ ] Vocabulary: synonyms, antonyms, one-word substitutions
- [ ] Error spotting; fill in the blanks

## Online-assessment angle
Do not overspend time here; it is usually the least discriminating section.

## Interview angle
Written communication quality shows in your emails and resume too.
