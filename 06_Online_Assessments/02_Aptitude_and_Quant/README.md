# Aptitude & Quantitative

> Folder: `06_Online_Assessments/02_Aptitude_and_Quant`

## Why this matters
Gatekeeper section at service-based companies and many product OAs. Pure speed-and-accuracy work.

## Must-know checklist
- [ ] Percentages, profit & loss, discount
- [ ] Ratio, proportion, mixtures, partnership
- [ ] Time-speed-distance; trains, boats
- [ ] Time and work; pipes and cisterns
- [ ] Simple & compound interest
- [ ] Averages, ages
- [ ] Permutations, combinations, probability
- [ ] Number system: divisibility, remainders, LCM/HCF, unit digit
- [ ] Mensuration and basic geometry
- [ ] Data interpretation: tables, bar/pie charts

## Online-assessment angle
Target 45-60 seconds per question. Learn the shortcut method for every topic, not the textbook method.

## Interview angle
Occasionally used as a warm-up puzzle in interviews.
