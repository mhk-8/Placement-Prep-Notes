# Timed Mock Logs

> Folder: `06_Online_Assessments/06_Timed_Mock_Logs`

## Why this matters
The feedback loop. Without logging, you repeat the same mistakes for months.

## Must-know checklist
- [ ] Date, platform, duration, sections attempted
- [ ] Score and estimated cutoff
- [ ] Per-question: solved? time taken? what blocked you?
- [ ] Root cause of each miss: concept gap / careless bug / time mismanagement / panic
- [ ] One concrete action per mock, carried into the next week's plan

## Online-assessment angle
Do at least one full-length timed mock every week from week 4 onward. Two per week in the last month.

## Interview angle
Patterns in your misses tell you exactly what to revise before an interview.
