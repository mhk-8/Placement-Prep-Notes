# Logical Reasoning & Puzzles

> Folder: `06_Online_Assessments/03_Logical_Reasoning_and_Puzzles`

## Why this matters
Tests structured thinking under pressure; puzzles also appear in interviews at quant and product firms.

## Must-know checklist
- [ ] Series: number, letter, figure
- [ ] Blood relations, directions, coding-decoding
- [ ] Seating arrangement (linear, circular), puzzles with constraints tables
- [ ] Syllogisms, statements & assumptions
- [ ] Clocks and calendars
- [ ] Classic interview puzzles: bridge crossing, egg drop, weighing balls, burning ropes, 100 prisoners, water jugs, poisoned wine

## Online-assessment angle
Arrangement puzzles are time sinks — attempt them last.

## Interview angle
Think aloud and structure the state space. The reasoning is graded, not just the answer.
