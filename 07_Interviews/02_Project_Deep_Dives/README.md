# Project Deep Dives

> Folder: `07_Interviews/02_Project_Deep_Dives`

## Why this matters
For an M.Tech candidate, projects and thesis are often 40-50% of interview time. This is where you can be genuinely expert.

## Must-know checklist
- [ ] One file per project: problem, why it mattered, your specific contribution, architecture, tech choices with alternatives rejected, metrics achieved, hardest bug, what you would do differently, how you would scale it
- [ ] A 60-second version and a 5-minute version of each
- [ ] Anticipated follow-ups and their answers
- [ ] Honest limitations — stating them yourself builds credibility
- [ ] Your thesis explained to a non-specialist in 3 minutes

## Online-assessment angle
Not applicable.

## Interview angle
Every technical claim in your project story must survive a 'why' asked three times. Test that yourself.
