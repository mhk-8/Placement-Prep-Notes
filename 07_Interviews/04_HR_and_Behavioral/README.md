# HR & Behavioral

> Folder: `07_Interviews/04_HR_and_Behavioral`

## Why this matters
Usually the final gate. Rarely wins an offer, frequently loses one.

## Must-know checklist
- [ ] Tell me about yourself — a structured 90-second answer, rehearsed not memorised
- [ ] Why this company, why this role, why you
- [ ] Strengths and a genuine weakness with a remediation story
- [ ] STAR stories: conflict, failure, leadership, tight deadline, learning something fast, disagreeing with a decision, going beyond scope
- [ ] Handling: relocation, bond/service agreement, notice, competing offers, salary expectations
- [ ] Gaps, backlogs, or a low grade — a calm, factual, non-defensive answer
- [ ] Questions about long-term plans and higher studies

## Online-assessment angle
Some companies include a psychometric/situational-judgement section in the OA — answer consistently, not strategically.

## Interview angle
Specific beats impressive. A small real story told concretely outperforms a grand vague one.
