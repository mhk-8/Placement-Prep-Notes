# Post-Interview Debriefs

> Folder: `07_Interviews/06_Post_Interview_Debriefs`

## Why this matters
The compounding asset. Ten debriefs make the eleventh interview substantially easier.

## Must-know checklist
- [ ] Company, role, round, date, interviewer's background
- [ ] Every question asked, verbatim as far as you recall
- [ ] What you answered and how it landed
- [ ] What you fumbled and the correct answer, written out now
- [ ] Format and tone observations for the next round
- [ ] Outcome and any feedback received

## Online-assessment angle
Feeds directly back into `06_Online_Assessments/01_OA_Patterns_by_Company`.

## Interview angle
Write it the same day. Two days later you will remember a third of it.

## Suggested files in this folder
- `notes.md`
- `scripts-and-answers.md`
- `questions-asked.md`
- `action-items.md`
