
# Post-Interview Debrief — Template

> **The compounding asset.** Ten debriefs make the eleventh interview substantially easier: you
> stop being surprised, you stop repeating the same fumbles, and you build a company-specific
> question bank that no public resource has.
>
> ⚠️ **Write it the same day.** Within two hours you recall most of it; within two days you recall
> about a third, and the third you lose is disproportionately the part you got wrong.
>
> **Copy the block below into a new file** named `YYYY-MM-DD_<Company>_R<n>.md`.

## Thank-you notes ⭐

Optional in Indian campus placement and rarely expected, but harmless when done well and genuinely
useful for off-campus or referral processes.

```
If you send one:
  - Within 24 hours
  - Three or four sentences, no more
  - Reference ONE SPECIFIC thing from the conversation  ⭐ this is the whole point
  - Do not re-pitch yourself or add answers you wish you had given
  - Do not ask about the timeline

Example:
  "Thank you for the time today. The point you made about profiling being the constraint rather
   than the architecture matched something I ran into on my own GPU work, and it's given me
   something to think about. I enjoyed the conversation and would be glad to continue the process."
```
